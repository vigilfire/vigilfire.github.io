Fire equipment register & servicing

Retire the paper register.

Vigil Fire is a field-first register for South African fire equipment servicing companies. Your technicians service equipment on site — online or off — and the site register, service certificate and every SANS report come out the other side.

The problem

Compliance lives on clipboards and in someone's head

Service labels get overwritten. The register is a folder that's out of date the day it's printed. Pressure-test and recharge dates get missed. When an inspector or an insurer asks for three years of records, the scramble begins.

What Vigil Fire does

Everything a service visit produces, in one place

One combined form per unit — identity, service, inspection checklist, pressure test, recharge — no jumping between screens.

Site registers

Every site's equipment, current status and what's due — one tap to a multi-page printable register or a certificate of service.

Servicing & inspection

Type-specific checklists, auto next-service dates, recorded mass at service, and every-visit seal renewal — all on the one form.

Pressure & hydrostatic tests

5- and 10-year intervals tracked from the last test. Record the Annex G procedure, test pressure and result — with the technician's SAQCC number.

Recharge workflow

Flag a unit for recharge and it stays on the Due list until it's back and logged — pressure before and after, medium and checklist. The register won't generate while a recharge is outstanding.

Monthly inspections

The SANS 10105-1 responsible-person walk-through, per site, with a printable rolling 12-month log to leave on the clipboard.

Calibration register

Weekly checks of your workshop scales and pressure gauges against reference instruments (SANS 1475-1 Annex A), assignable to a named technician.

Technicians & permissions

Add technician and trainee logins in-app — no console fiddling. SAQCC numbers on every record, technicians assigned to their own sites, calibration rights granted per person, and a leaver deactivated without losing their history.

Letterheaded reports

Site register, certificate of service, pressure-test report, recharge report and monthly log — headed with your company profile and authorised signature. Print, save as PDF, or email straight to the site, with a record of every document sent.

Records that hold up

An immutable audit trail behind every change, plus a scheduled full backup — so the SANS three-year retention requirement is genuinely met.

Nothing slips

The Due list is the day's work

Every site, every unit, one screen: overdue services, pressure tests coming up, CO₂ weight checks, hose-reel replacements and outstanding recharges — sorted so the most urgent is first.

  • Condemned units are marked, dated and reason-coded per SANS 1475-1 clause 5.2.6, then dropped from the live count
  • Replacement units link back to what they replaced
  • A site tagged "monthly inspection" opens straight to its check form
Built for the field

No signal in the basement? No problem.

Vigil Fire installs like an app and works fully offline. A technician can service an entire building with no reception; everything syncs the moment they're back on the network. An online/offline indicator tells them where they stand.

  • Installable on any phone or tablet — nothing to download from a store
  • Photos captured on site, resized and uploaded automatically
  • Roles keep technicians to their assigned sites; admins see everything
Bring on the next technician

The SAQCC trainee logbook, done properly

Register a trainee for their six months of supervised field training and the paper daily logbook moves into the app. The trainee logs each workshop or on-the-job day; only the registered technician who was actually there can sign it off, and a signed entry locks.

  • 33-point competency checklist — each one achieved only after four dated technician observations
  • Supervisors mark competencies from their own phone; trainees see progress but can't self-mark
  • Print the full logbook and competency record for the SAQCC EISA portfolio of evidence
How it works

Three steps, then it runs itself

Set up your company

Add your client sites and technicians, and enter your company letterhead and authorised signature once.

Service in the field

Technicians open a unit, work through the checklist, record the test or recharge, and move on — online or off.

Generate & file

Sign the site off, then print, email or PDF the register and certificate. Reports and the records backup are a tap away.

Written around the standards

Not a generic asset tracker

Every interval, checklist and record in Vigil Fire comes from the South African standards your work is judged against — so the app nudges you toward compliance instead of leaving it to memory.

SANS 1475-1 Reconditioning of portable and wheeled rechargeable fire extinguishers — servicing intervals, the hydrostatic-test procedure (Annex G), recharge requirements, condemning criteria, labelling and the three-year record.
SANS 10105-1 The use and control of fire-fighting equipment — the monthly responsible-person inspection and its on-site log.
SANS 543 & SANS 1475-1 Annex A Hose-reel replacement intervals, and weekly calibration of the workshop's production-control equipment against reference instruments.
